What Triggers Learning Agility?

DeRue identifies 'antecedents' — conditions that precede and enable learning agility in a given situation.

Person Factors
  • Personality (extraversion, openness)
  • Goal orientation
  • Prior skill level in the area
  • Tolerance for ambiguity
Context Factors
  • Organizational culture
  • Psychological safety
  • Whether creativity is promoted or stifled
  • Available support and resources
Behavioral Factors
  • Skill proficiency in the domain
  • Motivation and importance of the task
  • Level of defensiveness
  • Openness to feedback
