When to Decide Faster

  • ➔ Reversible decisions
  • ➔ Low stakes / contained impact
  • ➔ Team is blocked waiting
  • ➔ Data won't improve with time

When to Slow Down

  • ➔ Irreversible consequences
  • ➔ Major financial commitment
  • ➔ People's careers or safety at stake
  • ➔ Legal or compliance implications
