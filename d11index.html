One-Way Doors

  • Irreversible or extremely costly to undo
  • Require thorough analysis and deliberation
  • Examples: selling a business, major relocations
  • Deserve more time and consultation
  • About 10% of your decisions
🚪🔄

Two-Way Doors

Analyze Shutter

Two-Way Doors

  • Easily reversible with low cost
  • Should be made quickly and iterated on
  • Examples: testing a new tool, trying a process
  • Overthinking these wastes time and energy
  • About 90% of your decisions
💡 Strategic Framework Alignment: Knowing the difference prevents organizational friction. Guard one-way doors with deep consensus metrics, while running two-way decisions as rapid testing loops.
