Clear Decision Rights

Ambiguity about who decides is the primary source of organizational indecision.

Cures Deployed: 0 / 5
Efficiency Rating: 100%

Organizational Pathology Log

"Our business units are frozen because four different department heads hold overlapping veto authority over the exact same project milestones."

Root Causes of Indecision
  • Complex structures and overlapping responsibilities
  • Informal power dynamics and politics
  • Lack of trust among senior leaders
  • Fear of failure or accountability
  • Conflict avoidance in political environments
Solutions
  • Set decision deadlines for every open item
  • Separate veto from advisory input
  • Make decision authority explicit and visible
  • Map decision rights using RAPID or similar
  • Establish escalation paths with time limits
