Bob Iger — Disney's Transformation

The Decisive Move

When Iger became Disney CEO in 2006, the animation studio was struggling. Rather than waiting for internal solutions, he made a bold call: acquire Pixar for $7.4 billion.

This single decision launched a strategic transformation that would include Marvel, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox — turning Disney into the world's content powerhouse.

Against the Current

Nooyi shifted PepsiCo toward healthier offerings despite intense skepticism from industry analysts. She invested heavily in bottled water, baked snacks, and reduced-sugar products while competitors clung to traditional formulations.

By her 2018 departure, PepsiCo was positioned ahead of market health trends — validating her decisive stance against conventional wisdom.

Lessons in Decisiveness

  • Trust your conviction even when experts disagree
  • Align decisions with long-term trends, not short-term pressure
  • Invest through the skepticism — results validate the strategy
  • Maintain decisive course despite quarterly pressures

Decisive Crisis Management

When GM faced a deadly ignition switch defect, Barra didn't hesitate or deflect. She immediately acknowledged the company's failures, initiated a transparent investigation, and allocated $900 million for claims.

This decisive response restored public confidence in GM's integrity and set the foundation for her equally bold pivot toward electric vehicles.
