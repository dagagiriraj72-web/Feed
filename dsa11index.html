5 Principles of a Decisive Culture

01
Clarity Over Consensus
One decider, clear roles, explicit criteria
02
Speed Over Perfection
Move fast on reversible decisions, deliberate on irreversible ones
03
Accountability Over Blame
Own outcomes, learn from mistakes, never punish well-reasoned risk
04
Action Over Analysis
Default to doing — the best insights come from execution, not meetings
05
Learning Over Certainty
Treat every decision as a hypothesis to test, not a bet to win
