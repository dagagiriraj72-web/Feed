Decisiveness vs. Impulsiveness

Decisiveness

Strategic Path
  • Considers key information before acting
  • Accepts reasonable risk deliberately
  • Commits with awareness of trade-offs
  • Reflects briefly, then moves forward
  • Takes ownership of the outcome

Impulsiveness

Reactive Path
  • Reacts before gathering relevant facts
  • Ignores risk or consequences entirely
  • Chooses to avoid discomfort, not to solve
  • Skips reflection, regrets later
  • Blames circumstances for poor results
