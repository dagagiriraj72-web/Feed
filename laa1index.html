🧠 Ability

Learning Ability
"How quickly you learn"
Learning Ability reflects a person's capacity to absorb information, understand concepts, and perform analytical tasks efficiently.

Cognitive Intelligence

IQ and mental processing capability.

Fast Learning

Quickly understands new information.

Academic Strength

Analytical and theoretical capability.

Relatively Stable

Tends to remain consistent over time.

Learning Agility
"What you do when you don't know what to do"
Learning Agility is the ability to adapt, experiment, and apply learning successfully in unfamiliar situations.

Behavioral Flexibility

Adjusts actions based on changing conditions.

Adaptability

Thrives in new and uncertain environments.

Knowledge Transfer

Applies lessons from past experiences.

Growth Potential

Can be developed through practice.
