Learning Agility vs. Learning Ability

A critical distinction for talent management
Viewed: 0/5

Learning Ability

  • Cognitive intelligence (IQ)
  • How quickly you absorb information
  • Academic and analytical capacity
  • Relatively fixed trait
  • Necessary but not sufficient

Learning Agility

  • Behavioral flexibility
  • How you apply learning in novel situations
  • Range of adaptive capabilities
  • Can be developed over time
  • The differentiator for leadership potential
