Type 1 Framework Parameters

  • Irreversible and high-stakes
  • Require full analysis and deliberation
  • Involve cross-functional input
  • ~10% of organizational decisions

Example: Major acquisition, market exit

TYPE 2

Two-Way Doors
🗏

Type 2 Framework Parameters

  • Reversible and lower-stakes
  • Should be made quickly and locally
  • Delegate to closest team
  • ~90% of organizational decisions

Example: Pricing test, tool selection, feature rollout
