Common Decision Traps

Decision Trap Profile • Category 01

Analysis Paralysis

Endlessly researching and weighing options without ever committing to a choice.

Decision Trap Profile • Category 02

Perfectionism

Waiting for the ideal option that doesn't exist, while good-enough options expire.

Decision Trap Profile • Category 03

Choice Overload

Too many options create anxiety. Fewer choices often lead to better decisions.

Decision Trap Profile • Category 04

Procrastination

Delaying the decision feels safe but compounds stress and limits options.
