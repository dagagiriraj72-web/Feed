Common Decision Traps

Cognitive Trap 01

Anchoring Bias

Over-relying on the first piece of information encountered, even if irrelevant.

Cognitive Trap 02

Sunk Cost Fallacy

Continuing a failing path because of past investment rather than future potential.

Cognitive Trap 03

Groupthink

Prioritizing consensus over critical evaluation of alternatives.

Cognitive Trap 04

Status Quo Bias

Defaulting to existing conditions because change feels risky.

Cognitive Trap 05

Confirmation Bias

Seeking only information that supports a preferred conclusion.

Cognitive Trap 06

Analysis Paralysis

Endlessly researching without ever committing to action.
