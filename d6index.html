The 10-10-10 Rule

Ask yourself three questions to cut through emotional fog:

10
Minutes

How will I feel about this decision ten minutes from now?

10
Months

How will I feel about it ten months from now?

10
Years

How will I feel about it ten years from now?

Temporal Horizon Context: Immediate Impact

Immediate Consequences Evaluation

Identify the initial surge of emotional spikes or situational anxiety. Enforcing a micro pause here prevents rash, defensive reactions on the spot and cools immediate visceral adrenaline drivers before finalizing execution.

Temporal Horizon Context: Intermediate Development

Mid-Term Structural Alignment

Evaluate if current team contentions or friction steps will matter once standard project cycles hit production maturity. This pivot separates temporary operational struggles from lasting high-value team milestones.

Temporal Horizon Context: Macro Strategy

Long-Term Milestone Retrospective

Analyze if this singular issue registers as a historical turning point or if you are over-indexing on temporary pride. Focusing on the macro scale builds robust relationship capital and systemic clarity.
