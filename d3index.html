Cognitive Biases That Derail Decisions

IB Group
Behavioral Pattern • 01

Anchoring

The first piece of information we encounter disproportionately shapes our judgment, even if it's irrelevant.

Behavioral Pattern • 02

Confirmation Bias

We seek information that supports what we already believe, ignoring evidence to the contrary.

Behavioral Pattern • 03

Availability Bias

Recent or vivid events dominate our thinking, making rare risks feel common and likely.
