Tools for Faster Decisions

Decision Tool 01

RAPID Framework

Assign roles: Recommend, Agree, Perform, Input, Decide. Eliminates confusion.

Decision Tool 02

Decision Matrix

Score options against weighted criteria. Makes trade-offs visible and objective.

Decision Tool 03

10/10/10 Test

How will you feel about this in 10 minutes, 10 months, 10 years? Adds temporal perspective.

Decision Tool 04

2-Minute Rule

If the decision takes less than 2 minutes of analysis, decide immediately. Don’t defer.

Decision Tool 05

Red Team Review

Assign someone to argue against. If the decision survives challenge, it’s robust.

Decision Tool 06

Coin Flip Test

Flip a coin. Your gut reaction to the result reveals what you actually want.
