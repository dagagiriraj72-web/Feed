When your values are clear, decisions become simple.

  • Write down your top five personal and professional values
  • Score each option against those values before choosing
  • Eliminate choices that violate a core value — no negotiation
  • Revisit your values quarterly to ensure they still reflect who you are
  • Use values as a tiebreaker when options seem equal

Parkinson's law applies to decisions too — they expand to fill the time you give them.

⏱️
Low Impact < 60 seconds

What to eat, what to wear, which route to take

📅
Medium Impact < 24 hours

Team scheduling, purchase decisions, replying to proposals

📈
High Impact < 1 week

Career moves, major investments, strategic pivots

A good framework reduces the cognitive load of every decision.

  • Apply the 10-10-10 rule for emotional clarity (covered in Section 4)
  • Use a decision matrix for multi-criteria choices
  • Try pre-mortem analysis: imagine the decision failed — what went wrong?
  • Apply satisficing: choose the first option that meets your criteria
  • Reserve deep analysis only for truly irreversible, high-stakes calls
