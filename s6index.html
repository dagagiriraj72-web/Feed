Four Actions Framework Strategic Executive Summary

The Four Actions Framework uncovers cost reduction paths while driving differentiation to successfully break old trade-off curves.

1. Eliminate

Strips away competitive factors that industries historically take for granted but have completely lost transactional or functional utility.

2. Reduce

Forces corporate products or operational parameters well below standard margins to minimize unnecessary system expenditure points.

3. Raise

Elevates specific high-impact delivery parameters high above industry norms to address core consumer pain points effectively.

4. Create

Introduces net-new value metrics, platforms, or product features that direct market competitors have never historically provided.
