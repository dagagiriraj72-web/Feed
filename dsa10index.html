Decision Velocity: Analyzing

Analysis Obstacle Diagnostic

"Projects are stalling because teams endlessly deliberate over options without any formal closure date."

Set Decision Deadlines

Every decision gets a time limit. No deadline = no decision. Urgency drives clarity.

Apply the 70% Rule

If you have 70% of the information, decide. Waiting for 90%+ means you're already too late.

Classify Before Deciding

Is this a one-way or two-way door? Match your process intensity to the decision's reversibility.

Default to Action

Make 'yes' the default for reversible decisions. Require a reason to say no, not a reason to say yes.

Conduct Pre-Mortems

Before deciding, imagine the decision failed. What went wrong? Address those risks, then commit.

Embrace Imperfection

A good decision made today beats a perfect decision made next month. Course-correct as you learn.
