Your 30-Day Action Plan

Week 1

Awareness

Identify 3 areas where you could take more initiative
Journal daily observations about your proactivity
Set one SMART goal for the month
Week 2

Small Actions

Volunteer for one new task at work
Practice the 5-second rule three times daily
Share one idea in a team meeting
Week 3

Momentum

Take ownership of a process improvement
Seek feedback on your initiative efforts
Build one new professional connection
Week 4

Commitment

Reflect on your progress and adjust goals
