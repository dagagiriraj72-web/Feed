Decision Debt

Unmade decisions compound like financial debt — silently and relentlessly

Every stalled approval sits in your head like open browser tabs.

Decision debt is the cognitive load created by unresolved decisions. The more you carry, the less space you have for strategic thinking.

The Compounding Effect

  • Hiring is delayed
  • Revenue opportunities expire
  • Team morale dips
  • Competitive advantages erode
  • Innovation pipeline stalls
