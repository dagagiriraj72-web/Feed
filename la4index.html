Learning Agility out-predicts IQ and Experience for executive career success.

Decades of leadership research demonstrate that agile leaders do not rely strictly on static past recipes:

  • Quickly extract lessons from unfamiliar & high-stakes situations
  • Rapidly adapt their mental models when operating without playbooks
  • Solicit corrective feedback and apply it without defensiveness
  • Consistently outperform peers when facing first-time market shifts
Executive Reality: Past track records predict performance only when conditions repeat. Learning agility predicts performance when conditions change.
