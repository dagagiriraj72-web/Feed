Clarity of Purpose

Knowing your destination makes every choice along the way simpler and faster.

Establish clear, measurable goals that anchor every decision. When priorities compete, your north star breaks the tie.
Map your core values to decision criteria. This creates a repeatable filter that eliminates guesswork.
Share your decision rationale with the team. Transparent reasoning builds trust and empowers others to decide.

Informed Confidence

Confidence without information is recklessness. Information without confidence is paralysis. Great decision-makers live at the intersection.

Surround yourself with dissenting voices. The best decisions survive challenge.
Pattern recognition from past decisions is a valid input. Honor your instinct.
Define what "enough data" looks like before you start gathering it.
No decision will ever have 100% certainty. Learn to move forward with 70%.

Bias for Action

Applying operational velocity and intent to enterprise execution roadmaps.

1
Recognize

Identify the decision that needs to be made. Name it explicitly.

2
Timebox

Set a clear deadline. Most decisions don't improve with infinite deliberation.

3
Commit

Make the call. Announce it. Write it down. Remove the option to waffle.

4
Execute

Pour your energy into making the decision work, not second-guessing it.

5
Learn

Review outcomes. Extract lessons. Feed them into your next decision cycle.
