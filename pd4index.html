Decision Fatigue

The Silent Productivity Killer

The average executive makes over 35,000 decisions per day. Each one depleletes cognitive resources, leading to progressively poorer choices.

  • Procrastinating on important choices
  • Defaulting to the status quo
  • Making impulsive decisions late in the day
  • Avoiding decisions altogether
