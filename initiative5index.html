Volunteer for Challenges

Propose Solutions

Never bring a problem without at least one proposed solution. Show you have thought it through.

Core Competency • 03

Build Bridges

Connect people and ideas across teams. Initiative-takers are natural networkers and collaborators.

Core Competency • 04

Improve Processes

Look for inefficiencies in daily work and suggest better approaches. Small improvements compound.
