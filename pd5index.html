The Decision-Making Framework

Framework Milestone 01

DEFINE

What exactly needs to be decided? Frame the question clearly.

Framework Milestone 02

GATHER

Collect essential inputs. Consult 2–3 key perspectives.

Framework Milestone 03

EVALUATE

Weigh options against your criteria and values.

Framework Milestone 04

DECIDE

Choose. Document your rationale for future reference.

Framework Milestone 05

ACT

Communicate and execute with full commitment.

Framework Milestone 06

REVIEW

Assess outcomes. Iterate and improve.
