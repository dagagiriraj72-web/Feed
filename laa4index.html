The Research Journey

2012

DeRue, Ashford & Myers at University of Michigan identify Speed and Flexibility as two core dimensions of learning agility

2015

Burke and doctoral students at Columbia identify seven additional dimensions, creating the nine-cluster model

2015

Burke Learning Agility Inventory (Burke LAI) launched as a validated 360-degree assessment instrument

2017

Publication of Learning Agility: The Key to Leader Potential — the definitive guide to the framework

2022

Publication of Developing Learning Agility — practical activities for developing the 38 behaviors in work settings
