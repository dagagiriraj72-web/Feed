Decision Frameworks

Type 1 Decision

Heavy-Deliberation Strategic Framework

Reversibility: Irreversible or extremely costly to undo once committed.

Analysis Requirements: Require thorough, rigorous market analysis and extended cross-functional deliberation.

Real-World Examples: Selling an enterprise business unit, corporate infrastructure relocations.

Type 2 Decision

High-Velocity Operational Framework

Reversibility: Easily reversible with exceptionally low real-world resource costs.

Analysis Requirements: Should be made quickly, autonomously, and immediately iterated on in-field.

Real-World Examples: Testing an internal software tool, trying an experimental workflow variation.
