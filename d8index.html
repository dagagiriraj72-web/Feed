IRREVERSIBLE STRATEGY

Deep Analysis Required

  • Irreversible or extremely costly to undo once committed.
  • Requires thorough, methodical analysis and deep deliberation.
  • Core Examples: Selling a business, major relocations, changing architectural foundations.
  • Deserves extended timelines, extensive consultation, and protective guardrails.
EASILY REVERSIBLE

High-Velocity Iteration

  • Easily reversible with remarkably low cost of failure.
  • Should be made quickly, using minimal committees, and iterated upon in production.
  • Core Examples: Testing a new software tool, experimenting with a process variant.
  • Overthinking these operational micro-steps systematically wastes team time and execution energy.
