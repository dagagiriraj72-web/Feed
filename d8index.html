Two-Way Door Decisions

Type 1 Decision

Irreversible Strategic Framework

  • Reversibility: Irreversible or extremely costly to undo.
  • Analysis Requirements: Require thorough, rigorous analysis and deep deliberation.
  • Real-World Examples: Selling a business unit, executing major geographic relocations.
  • Execution Velocity: Deserve more time, collaborative consultation, and conservative fallback plans.
Type 2 Decision

High-Velocity Operational Framework

  • Reversibility: Easily reversible with exceptionally low real-world costs.
  • Analysis Requirements: Should be made quickly, autonomously, and immediately iterated on.
  • Real-World Examples: Testing an internal software tool, trying a experimental workflow process.
  • Execution Velocity: Overthinking these lightweight operational parameters wastes vital team time and energy.
