RAPID in Practice

How clear decision roles eliminate ambiguity and accelerate execution

Challenge RAPID Solution Benefit
"Too many cooks" Assign one Recommender per decision Single accountability
"Decision by committee" Name one Decider with final authority Clear ownership
"Approval loops" Limit Agree roles to legal/regulatory Faster throughput
"Everyone has an opinion" Distinguish Input from Agree Focused feedback
"Who's doing it?" Assign Perform role at decision time Immediate execution
