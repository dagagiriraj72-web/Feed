OODA Loop in Business

Applying military-grade decision speed to competitive strategy

Observe
Monitor market signals, competitor moves, customer behavior, and internal metrics in real time
Orient
Contextualize data against your industry knowledge, organizational culture, and strategic position
Decide
Choose the optimal response — enter a market, pivot a product, adjust pricing — with conviction
Act
Execute swiftly and decisively, then immediately begin observing the results to start the next cycle
