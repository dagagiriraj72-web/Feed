Proactive vs Reactive

Proactive
  • Anticipates problems before they arise
  • Creates opportunities for growth
  • Takes ownership of outcomes
  • Focuses on what can be controlled
  • Drives change and innovation
Reactive
  • Responds only when problems appear
  • Waits for instructions to act
  • Blames circumstances for results
  • Focuses on what cannot be changed
  • Resists or avoids change
