Measuring Decision Effectiveness

Key metrics to track your decisiveness
Performance Matrix 01

Decision Velocity

Time from problem identification to committed action.

Target Threshold

<48 hrs for reversible decisions

Performance Matrix 02

Hit Rate

Percentage of decisions that achieve desired outcome.

Target Threshold

65–75% is excellent

Performance Matrix 03

Escalation Ratio

How often decisions get pushed up the chain.

Target Threshold

<15% of team decisions escalated

Performance Matrix 04

Reversal Rate

How often decisions need to be reversed or significantly changed.

Target Threshold

<10% for well-framed choices
